Three questions you ask every morning, answered on one screen.

What is blocked?

Stalled reviews, failing checks and work nobody has touched. Each comes with the milestone it holds up and the person it is waiting on.

What needs me?

Reviews you were asked for and changes requested on your own work, oldest first, each with one button for the next step.

Will we ship on time?

Every repository shows its next milestone, the due date and whether the work left fits the days left.

We read. We do not touch.

Dirigo needs to see issues, pull requests, reviews and check results. It never reads your code and cannot change anything. Every action you take happens on GitHub.
